# COACHFORWARD Performance Conversation Checklist (Simulated)

## Before
- [ ] Define one purpose for the conversation
- [ ] Gather two or three observable examples
- [ ] Review prior goals and commitments
- [ ] Choose time, place, and give advance notice

## During
- [ ] Open with purpose and positive intent
- [ ] Use the S-B-I-A structure
- [ ] Ask open questions; pause
- [ ] Summarize what you heard
- [ ] Agree on one specific action and a date

## After
- [ ] Document agreement
- [ ] Follow up within a week
- [ ] Recognize progress

## Reflection prompts
1. What did I do well?
2. What would I try differently?
3. What support do I need?
