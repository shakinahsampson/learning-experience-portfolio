# ELEVATE Orientation Workshop — Facilitator Guide

> SIMULATED PROJECT. Meridian Strategy Group is a fictional firm. This is an independent portfolio sample, not client work.

**Duration:** 3 hours (virtual or in person) | **Group size:** 8–16 | **Audience:** new consultants and new hires

## Learning objectives
By the end, participants will be able to:
1. **Describe** the 90-day onboarding journey and where to find each resource.
2. **Identify** three performance expectations for their role and how they are measured.
3. **Draft** a personal 30-day plan with at least two measurable goals.
4. **Select** the right channel to ask for help in four common situations.

## Facilitator preparation (T-5 days to T-1 hour)
- [ ] Confirm roster, accommodations requests, and room/virtual link (T-5)
- [ ] Review participant roles; adjust examples for each role (T-4)
- [ ] Test slides, breakout rooms, polls, and caption settings (T-2)
- [ ] Print or share job aids and the 30-day plan template (T-1)
- [ ] Brief the guest manager panelists on timing (10 min each) (T-1)
- [ ] Open the room 15 minutes early; display the welcome prompt (T-0)

## Agenda
| Time | Segment | Method | Facilitator notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| 0:00–0:15 | Welcome and norms | Whole group | Ask: "What would make the first 30 days a success for you?" Capture on a shared board. |
| 0:15–0:45 | The 90-day journey | Mini-lecture + map | Keep to 10 minutes; learners annotate their own timeline. |
| 0:45–1:25 | Expectations and performance | Case discussion | Use the Priya scenario; pairs rank expectations and name evidence. |
| 1:25–1:35 | Break | — | Stretch, caption and accessibility check. |
| 1:35–2:15 | How we get help | Role-play stations | Four stations rotate every 10 minutes. |
| 2:15–2:45 | Build your 30-day plan | Individual work + peer review | Use SMART goal template; peers give one strength, one question. |
| 2:45–3:00 | Close and commitments | Exit ticket | Collect Level 1 reaction (3 questions). |

## Facilitation tips
- Use cold-call only after pair talk. Normalize not knowing.
- Provide all key instructions in writing as well as aloud.
- Allow extra processing time; offer an alternative to speaking in groups.
- If energy dips, switch to a poll or stand-and-stretch.

## Debrief questions
- What surprised you about how success is measured?
- Which resource will you open first tomorrow?
- What is one question you still have, and who can answer it?

## After the session
Send resources within 24 hours, log attendance, and add exit-ticket themes to the improvement backlog.
