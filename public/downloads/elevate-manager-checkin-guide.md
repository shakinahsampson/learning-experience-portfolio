# ELEVATE Manager Check-In Guide (Simulated Sample)

Fictional scenario for Meridian Strategy Group. Adapt before real use.

## Weekly 1:1 structure (30 minutes)
1. **Connect (3 min)** — "How are you settling in?"
2. **Progress (7 min)** — Review last week's commitments against the 90-day plan.
3. **Obstacles (8 min)** — "What is slowing you down? What is unclear?"
4. **Feedback (7 min)** — One specific strength, one specific growth area, tied to observable behavior.
5. **Commit (5 min)** — Agree on two priorities for next week; record in the shared plan.

## Questions by phase
- **Week 1:** Who have you met? What is still unclear about expectations?
- **Days 8–30:** Which tasks feel familiar? Which need a model or practice?
- **Days 31–60:** What feedback have you received? What did you do with it?
- **Days 61–90:** What would you do differently on your next project? What support do you need next?

## Red flags to follow up
- Repeated missed commitments without explanation
- Reluctance to ask questions
- Unclear on how performance is measured
- Resources not provisioned (systems, access, buddy)

## Documentation
Record date, topics, commitments, and support requested. Keep notes factual and behavior-based.
