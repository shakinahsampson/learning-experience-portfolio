# INSIGHT360 — One-Page Executive Summary

**ALL DATA IS SYNTHETIC AND FICTIONAL.** 72 generated learner records across three fictional programs and six cohorts. No real organization, employee, or result is represented.

## Purpose
Show how an L&D leader would evaluate program effectiveness and decide next steps.

## Headline learning metrics (synthetic; see the live dashboard for filters)
- **Completion:** about 92% overall (66 of 72 enrolled).
- **Pre/post assessment:** average gain of about 26 points (59 → 85) among the 65 records with both scores.
- **Satisfaction:** average about 4.0 out of 5.
- **Application:** about half (≈52%) of completers with a manager observation show on-the-job application in manager reports.

## What these metrics mean
These are **learning metrics** (Kirkpatrick Levels 1–3). They do **not** prove a business outcome. We have not shown that the program caused any change in productivity, retention, or revenue.

## Data quality notes
Three known issues were injected on purpose to demonstrate checks: a completer with no post-score, a participation value above 100%, and a duplicate learner ID. They are excluded or flagged in analysis.

## Recommendations
1. **Investigate lower-engagement cohorts** with learner and manager conversations before changing content.
2. **Strengthen reinforcement:** add manager prompts at 30 days to raise on-the-job application.
3. **Fix data capture:** require post-assessment on completion; validate percentage fields.
4. **Plan a Level 4 study:** pair with a comparison group and a pre-defined business measure before claiming impact.

## Privacy
Use pseudonymous IDs, aggregate small groups (n < 10), and limit access to need-to-know.
