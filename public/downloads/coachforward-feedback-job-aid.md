# COACHFORWARD Manager Job Aid — Feedback Framework (Simulated)

Fictional learning experience for first-time people managers. Not a delivered corporate program.

## S-B-I-A Framework
- **Situation:** Where and when? "In Tuesday's client meeting…"
- **Behavior:** What did you observe? Facts only.
- **Impact:** What effect did it have?
- **Ask / Agree:** Invite their view, then agree on next steps.

## Example
"In Tuesday's client meeting (S), you interrupted the client twice (B). It made it hard for them to finish their point (I). What was happening for you? What could you try next time? (A)"

## Do / Avoid
| Do | Avoid |
|---|---|
| Be specific and timely | Vague labels ("unprofessional") |
| Ask before telling | Assuming motives |
| Agree on one next step | Listing many issues at once |
| Follow up within a week | Leaving it unresolved |

## Pre-conversation checklist
- [ ] I have a specific example.
- [ ] I can state the impact.
- [ ] I've chosen a private setting.
- [ ] I'm prepared to listen.
